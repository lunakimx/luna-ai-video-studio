# Fia & Theo Character Bible

Read `fia-theo-canon.json` for the baseline identity values. Keep episode staging, temporary prop ownership, lighting, and emotion separate from those values. This is a user-specific animation design record, not a claim about model accuracy or animal psychology.

## Authority and updates

Latest explicit user changes take priority, followed by the accepted episode ledger and approved identity references within their assigned roles. Do not promote an assistant proposal or an erroneous generated frame to canon. If a newer reference conflicts with a locked trait and approval is unclear, flag that conflict before a paid generation. Keep unaffected traits unchanged.

The JSON contains no uploaded asset IDs. A reference available in chat or on disk is not automatically an input to Newtake or another generator. Bind only the actual selected inputs, preserving their exact interface tags. Record which asset supplies identity, composition, pose, background, or audio. Do not imply that a text rule guarantees visible identity preservation.

## Body-relative markings

Theo's anatomical RIGHT forepaw alone has the white sock. It appears on viewer-left in an unmirrored frontal view; in a rear view of an untwisted body it appears on viewer-right. During a turn, follow the actual limb rather than a screen corner. The other forepaw and both hind paws remain cocoa brown. Cream body fur does not authorize extra white socks.

Never horizontally flip Theo to create the opposite direction. A camera-angle change must preserve the same anatomical marking. A review should distinguish an actual reference-mirroring error from an intentional reflection in glass. In a reflection, track the real cat and the reflected cat consistently rather than changing the real marking.

Hidden paws are UNVERIFIED, not automatically correct or missing. Inspect a visible view or a relevant nearby frame before approving a critical marking. Do not expose all four paws unnaturally just to make every pose easy to inspect.

## Identity and accessory ownership

Fia keeps long pearl-white opal fur with lavender and icy-blue accents, aqua-green eyes, one attached fluffy plume tail, and her vintage key with its attached opal teardrop. Lighting may tint the coat temporarily, but must not permanently recolor it or replace the accepted fur with flat saturated blue or purple.

Theo keeps the short plush cocoa/cream coat, honey-amber eyes, rounded build, one short thick feline tail, dark collar, brass keyhole bell, and separate deep-teal HOUSE-shaped pendant. The earlier word 'zipper' was an erroneous description, not an approved redesign. The bell and house pendant must not merge into one object, migrate to Fia, or disappear after the cats overlap.

Use the accepted images for exact proportions; do not invent percentage-based size ratios. Keep eyes attached to the face, with no eyes on the back of the head. Count four limbs, two ears, and one connected tail per cat when those parts can be inspected. Occlusion is not an extra or missing limb.

Accessories remain attached unless an approved beat explicitly removes, transfers, or drops them. In that case, record owner, attachment site, release, destination, and later state. A key cannot remain on Fia's neck and simultaneously be loose in Theo's mouth unless duplication is explicitly part of the story.

## Supporting cast and world rules

Read the accepted episode roster. Do not force every episode to contain only two cats, and do not automatically add a kitten, rival, or henchmen. Preserve the names and designs already approved for that episode, including explicit exits and return restrictions. Do not treat old and renamed versions of a recurring cat as two separate characters.

Distinguish supporting cats through their approved features and behavior. Do not redesign a known rival just to increase variety. Unknown traits remain unknown until useful evidence or user approval supplies them.

The opal key activates only for an approved magical beat. Use the accepted aqua-green/aurora effect, not an invented default gold trail. Define its actual source, destination, and effect on the scene. Fantasy does not automatically authorize combat, levitation, new powers, damaged clothes, or torn fabric. Ordinary affection and jealousy scenes remain feline scenes.
